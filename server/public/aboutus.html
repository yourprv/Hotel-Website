Our Story

Hotel Maxx was born from a vision to create Kohalpur's first truly world-class hospitality experience. Established in 2018, we set out to redefine luxury accommodation in the Banke district by blending contemporary design with the warm, authentic hospitality Nepal is renowned for.

Our founders, with decades of experience in the hospitality industry, recognized the need for a premium business hotel that could cater to both domestic and international travelers seeking comfort, convenience, and exceptional service.

Today, Hotel Maxx stands as a landmark of sophistication in Kohalpur, offering 4-star amenities including a rooftop swimming pool, state-of-the-art conference facilities, exquisite dining options, and personalized service that makes every guest feel truly valued.

Our Journey

Milestones in Hotel Maxx's development and growth

2016 - Vision & Planning

Concept development began with extensive market research and planning to create Kohalpur's first luxury business hotel.

2017 - Construction

Groundbreaking ceremony marked the beginning of construction with focus on sustainable building practices and modern design.

2018 - Grand Opening

Hotel Maxx officially opened its doors, introducing Kohalpur to a new standard of luxury accommodation and hospitality.

2019 - Awards & Recognition

Received "Best Business Hotel in Western Nepal" award and achieved 4-star rating from Nepal Tourism Board.

2021 - Expansion

Added new conference facilities and expanded rooftop amenities including the swimming pool and terrace bar.

2023 - Sustainability Initiatives

Implemented comprehensive green practices including solar power, water conservation, and local community partnerships.
