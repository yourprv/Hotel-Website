About Hotel Maxx Kohalpur

Hotel Maxx stands as a beacon of luxury and comfort in the heart of Kohalpur, Banke district, offering an unparalleled hospitality experience for both business and leisure travelers.

Our hotel combines modern amenities with traditional Nepalese warmth, creating a welcoming atmosphere where every guest feels valued and cared for. With our prime location, you're never far from Kohalpur's key attractions and business centers.

At Hotel Maxx, we believe that true luxury lies in the details. From our thoughtfully designed rooms to our exceptional dining experiences and personalized service, every aspect of your stay is crafted to exceed your expectations.
